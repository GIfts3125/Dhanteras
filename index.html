Dhanteras for Laxmi

॥ शुभ धनतेरस ॥Dhanteras 2026

Laxmi

आप मेरे दिल की लक्ष्मी हो

Tonight the whole world will celebrate Dhanteras, worshipping Laxmi Maa. But the Laxmi of my heart is you.

Aap ho bachchi, aap hi mere dhan aur khushi ka origin ho. Mera har sukh aapse aata hai, aur har dukh aapke aane se chala jaata hai.

Bas aap ho jisse main apna sab kuch maanta hoon. Aap hi se ye Suraj hai.

Always yours, Suraj

Happy Dhanteras,
Mere Dil Ki Laxmi

Three gold coins for you

My wishes for you this Dhanteras

  • धन

    Dhan

    May your hands always be full, and may what we earn go to the things that make you happy.

  • आरोग्य

    Aarogya

    On Dhanteras we remember Lord Dhanvantari. I pray for your good health, today and every year after.

  • सुख

    Sukh

    May every festival find us together, laughing in the same room.

शुभ धनतेरस,
